# Benzinga research (accessed 2026-11-13)
## Direct News API
- **[V]** `GET https://api.benzinga.com/api/v2/news`; `token` query param (spec says the scheme is a query token; its prose says "Bearer" — conflict **[U]**). Params: `page` (0–100000), `pageSize` (default 15, **max 100**), `displayOutput` headline|abstract|full, `date`, `dateFrom`, `dateTo` (yyyy-mm-dd), `updatedSince`, `publishedSince` (unix UTC), `sort` (`created:desc` default; id|created|updated), `tickers` (≤50), `channels`, `topics`, `authors`, `content_types`, `importanceRank`, `region`; `cusips` needs a licence agreement. Source: [Get news items](https://docs.benzinga.com/api-reference/news-api/get-news-items.md).
- **[V]** Fields: `id`, `author`, `title`, `teaser`, `body`, `url`, `created`, `updated`, `image[]`, `channels[]`, `tags[]`, `stocks[]` (name, cusip, isin, exchange, gics, sector), `importance_rank`, `original_id`. **Timestamps documented as RFC 2822 "GMT-4"** — a fixed -04:00 offset; whether it is really fixed in winter (EST) is **[U]** and must be measured.
- **[V]** "Pagination for the News, Calendar, and Signals APIs cannot return result sets with more than 10,000 items" — narrow by date/ticker and combine ([Introduction](https://docs.benzinga.com/introduction/introduction.md)). Errors 400/401/404 `no_data_found`/499/500.
- **[V]** Access: "contact … licensing@benzinga.com" — **no self-serve key, no free trial, no price stated** on the page. Rate limits not stated. Historical depth on the direct API: **[U]**.
