## 3. Rules we agree to

- **Authorship is earned, not assigned.** Each author must have made a substantive contribution, reviewed the final manuscript, and be able to explain their part. Order is revisited at each gate if contributions shift.
- **Negative results are published.** If the main hypothesis fails, the paper is still written and submitted.
- **No result is presented externally** (slides, CV, interview) before it has passed the evaluation harness and been reviewed by one other author.
- **Venue and timing** are decided jointly; the advisor has final say on submission readiness.
- **Code** is released under the repository's MIT licence at submission; licensed data is not redistributed.
- **Use on CVs:** each person may list the project and describe their own contribution accurately from day one.
- **If someone leaves** the project, their merged work stays, and they are an author if they still meet the first rule, otherwise acknowledged.
